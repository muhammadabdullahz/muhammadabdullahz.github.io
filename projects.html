Projects

Empirical work in macroeconomic forecasting, causal inference and business intelligence. Replications of published research, independent analyses and interactive dashboards. Code is on GitHub.

Macroeconomic Forecasting & Time Series

Vector autoregressions, local projections and machine learning methods applied to inflation, unemployment, output and monetary policy.

Vector Autoregressions: Inflation, Unemployment & Monetary Policy

Replication of Stock and Watson (2001) with a quarterly U.S. VAR(4). Applies Granger causality tests, impulse response functions, forecast error variance decomposition, structural identification and out-of-sample forecasting to study macroeconomic dynamics and policy shocks.

PythonVARGranger CausalityIRFsFEVD

Local Projections: Monetary Policy Transmission

Replication of Jordà and Taylor on monetary policy transmission. Estimates CPI and unemployment responses to Romer-Romer shocks using local projections (LP) and LP-IV, with Frisch-Waugh-Lovell residualisation, 2SLS, Newey-West HAC inference, joint Wald tests and Bonferroni-adjusted confidence bands.

PythonLocal ProjectionsLP-IVHAC InferenceMonetary Policy

Forecasting Industrial Production with Ridge, Lasso & Trees

Forecasts U.S. industrial production growth from 120+ monthly FRED-MD indicators. Models are tuned with time-series cross-validation and evaluated out of sample against AR(2) and historical-mean benchmarks at 1-, 3-, 6- and 12-month horizons.

Pythonscikit-learnRidgeLassoRegression Trees

Phillips Curve & Inflation Forecasting

Analyses U.S. inflation and labor-market conditions with FRED data and replicates selected Stock and Watson (2008) forecasting methods. Implements recursive autoregressive and moving-average models with log-differencing, annualisation and multi-horizon forecasts.

PythonstatsmodelsAR / MA ModelsPhillips CurveFRED

Causal Inference & Policy Analysis

Quasi-experimental and instrumental variable designs for estimating the effects of policies and prices, with attention to identifying assumptions.

Minimum Wage and Employment: Difference-in-Differences

Python replication of Card and Krueger (1994). Reconstructs descriptive statistics, estimates DID and OLS models, examines treatment effects using wage-gap measures and checks robustness with alternative full-time-equivalent (FTE) employment definitions.

PythonDifference-in-DifferencesOLSRobustness Checks

CRA Designation and Mortgage Lending: Regression Discontinuity

Independent analysis of whether the Community Reinvestment Act low- and moderate-income label, assigned to tracts below 80% of area median family income, changes home-purchase lending. Built from 2023 HMDA and FFIEC data, with design checks, placebo cutoffs, alternative specifications and a bank versus non-bank split.

PythonRDDHMDAPlacebo TestsFair Lending

Automobile Demand: Instrumental Variables & 2SLS

Independent analysis of U.S. automobile demand using 1971–1990 BLP data. Compares IV, LIML and OLS to correct price endogeneity from unobserved quality, and assesses instrument strength and validity with first-stage F-statistics, weak-IV and overidentification tests.

Python2SLSLIMLWeak IV TestsDemand Estimation
