About

My work spans macroeconomic time series, causal inference, and market strategy, from forecasting inflation and output, to measuring what a policy actually changed, to informing how startups enter and grow in new markets.

Drawing on policy advisory work at the United Nations, business development and go-to-market in UAE startups, and economics research at the Centre for Behavioral Institutional Design, I aim to contribute to economic consulting and strategy work that gives decisions a firm foundation.
